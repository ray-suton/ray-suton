## Research

**In progress**

- **Phase3Lock: Three-Phase Locking for Efficient Diffusion Language Models**: with Prof. Zhiqiang Shen and team, targeting ICML 2027. Speeds up diffusion language models by deciding separately when to commit to a token, when to reuse earlier representations, and when to compute, matching full-compute answer quality while improving efficiency.
- **LLM Reasoning Injection and Interruptible Reasoning: Selective Update Acceptance**: with Prof. Zangir Iklassov. Tests whether reasoning models accept valid, authorized updates mid-inference while rejecting false claims and adversarial instructions; to our knowledge, the first benchmark to test both together.

**Under review**

- **EoKono: Benchmarking LLM's Understanding of Esperanto Language and Culture**: coauthor, ACL Rolling Review. 23 language models, 2,667 questions reviewed by nine qualified speakers.

**Workshop papers**

- **[Metric Choice Determines Semantic Geometry in LLM Hidden States](https://github.com/ray-suton/LLM_Expansion)**: first author, ICML 2026 Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PygdYDVgQc)). Raw L2 tracks norm growth across layers, while centered cosine reveals semantic, topic, and category structure.
- **[Deadline-Valid Replay: Anytime-Valid Testing for LLM Inference Policies](https://github.com/ray-suton/Deadline_Valid)**: first author, ICML 2026 Hypothesis Testing Workshop ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=KcqIBf7un6)). Separates apparent leaderboard gaps from certified policy-level advantages.
- **[SymStep: Symbolic Step Verification for Logical Reasoning](https://openreview.net/forum?id=THDsMgkDaj)**: coauthor, IJCAI-ECAI 2026 NILA Workshop. Step-wise symbolic verification with constraint-guided LLM reasoning; 97% accuracy on a ZebraLogicBench subset.
